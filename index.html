SPACE TO ESCAPE. TOOLS TO SURVIVE.

The 3,200-unit arena gives you longer escape routes. The camera automatically widens when fighters separate and closes in when they meet. Hold F to reduce frontal damage; raise your guard just before impact for a perfect parry. Stamina drains while guarding and recovers when you release. Press E to evade in your movement direction, or step backward when standing still. Y-Man can guard and dodge too. His new crescent, thorn volley, and telegraphed spirit flame can all be countered or avoided.

POWER DECIDES THE CLASH.

When opposing attacks meet, equal power cancels both before either can deal damage. Higher power destroys the weaker attack and carries on at full strength. A power I Cross Strike cancels a normal blast; use power II or higher against Y-Man’s heavy blasts. The power labels in your arsenal show each move’s level.

THE CHALLENGE

Choose how fiercely Y-Man hunts.

Tap jump for a short hop, or hold for full height. Jump again in midair to double jump with a ghost trail. Press U to charge a piercing High Laser; face your opponent before firing. Land attacks to charge your X-Burst. Dash through blasts and use the upper platforms to gain an advantage. Falling costs health and returns you to the arena.
